Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 01/05/2023

Last Updated: 11/11/2025

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Ledgerslogic collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our website or services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect:

  • Personal Information: Name, email, phone number, address, company name.
  • Financial Information: Accounting records, invoices, and related data provided for our services.
  • Technical Information: IP address, browser type, and usage analytics for website improvement.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Provide and manage our accounting, tax, and advisory services.
  • Communicate project updates and deliverables.
  • Improve website experience and service efficiency.
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data.

We may share data only with:

  • Authorized employees and subcontractors bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • Third-party service providers (e.g., QuickBooks, Google Workspace) essential to service delivery.
  • Government or regulatory bodies, if required by law.

4. Data Retention

We retain records only as long as necessary for professional, legal, and compliance purposes, after which they are securely deleted.

5. Data Security

We use encryption, secure servers, and restricted access to protect your information.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure — you acknowledge this inherent risk.

6. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to enhance user experience, track analytics, and remember user preferences.

You can manage or disable cookies in your browser settings.

7. International Data Transfers

If you are based outside India, your data may be transferred and processed in India or the United States.

By using our services, you consent to such transfers in compliance with applicable laws.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications.
  • Request data portability (in applicable regions like the EU/US).

To exercise these rights, contact: keshav@ledgerslogic.com

9. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted on our website with a revised “Last Updated” date.
