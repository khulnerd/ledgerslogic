The Ultimate Checklist for Your First ROC Annual Filing

Oct 23, 2025 · 210 views

Congratulations! You’ve successfully navigated the challenges of your first year in business. You’ve built a product, won clients, and managed operations. Now, it’s time to tackle a crucial, non-negotiable aspect of running a formal business entity in India: your first Annual Filing with the Registrar of Companies (ROC).

For many first-time entrepreneurs, this process can seem daunting, filled with unfamiliar forms and strict deadlines. But it doesn’t have to be.

Think of it as your company’s annual “health report” to the government. It demonstrates that your business is compliant, transparent, and in good standing. Neglecting this is not an option—it leads to severe penalties and can even result in your company being declared “defunct.”

This checklist will demystify the process and guide you through everything you need for a smooth and successful filing.

Why Is ROC Filing So Important?

Before diving into the “what,” let’s understand the “why.”

  1. Legal Mandate: It is required by law under the Companies Act, 2013, and the Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008.
  2. Avoid Heavy Penalties: The penalty for late filing is ₹100 per day, per form, with no upper limit. This can quickly run into lakhs.
  3. Maintain Active Status: Consistent failure to file can lead the ROC to strike your company’s name from the register.
  4. Builds Credibility: A clean compliance record is essential for securing bank loans, attracting investors, and winning large contracts.

The Core Filing Checklist: Pvt Ltd vs. LLP

The required forms depend on your business structure. Here are the essential annual filings for both Private Limited Companies and LLPs.

RequirementPrivate Limited Company (Pvt Ltd)Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)Purpose / Key Details
Annual Financial StatementsForm AOC-4Form 8Submits your audited Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss Account. It’s a complete report of your finances.
Annual ReturnForm MGT-7 / MGT-7AForm 11A snapshot of your company’s information as of the financial year-end (directors, shareholders, etc.).
Director/Partner KYCForm DIR-3 KYCAn annual verification for all individuals holding a Director Identification Number (DIN).
Return of DepositsForm DPT-3Not ApplicableCertifies that the company has not accepted any deposits or details any outstanding loans/advances.
Income Tax ReturnForm ITR-6Form ITR-5Filed with the Income Tax Department, separate from ROC filings, but a mandatory annual compliance.

Note: MGT-7A is a simplified annual return form for One Person Companies (OPCs) and Small Companies.

Your Step-by-Step Action Plan: Before You File

Filing the forms is the final step. The real work happens in the preparation.

  1. Finalize Your Books of Accounts — This is the foundation. Ensure every single transaction for the financial year (April 1st to March 31st) is recorded, categorized, and reconciled. This includes bank statements, invoices, and expense receipts.
  2. Prepare Financial Statements — From your finalized books, prepare the core financial statements: the Balance Sheet, the Profit and Loss (P&L) Statement, and the Cash Flow Statement.
  3. Appoint an Auditor (For Pvt Ltd) — All Private Limited Companies, regardless of turnover, must have their financial statements audited by a practicing Chartered Accountant. This is a mandatory prerequisite for filing Form AOC-4.
  4. Hold a Board Meeting (For Pvt Ltd) — Convene a formal meeting of the Board of Directors to approve the audited financial statements, the Board’s Report, and the Annual Return.
  5. Conduct the Annual General Meeting (AGM) (For Pvt Ltd) — The AGM must be held within six months from the end of the financial year. In this meeting, the company’s shareholders officially adopt the audited financial statements.
  6. Gather All Required Documents — This includes the signed financial statements, the Auditor’s Report, the Board’s Report, details of all directors and shareholders, and Digital Signature Certificates (DSCs) for the authorized directors/partners.
  7. File the e-Forms on the MCA Portal — Once all the above steps are complete, you or your professional consultant can proceed to fill and upload the required e-Forms on the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) portal.

Don’t Let Compliance Become a Crisis

Your first ROC annual filing sets the precedent for the financial discipline of your company. While it’s a process with many moving parts, it’s entirely manageable with a systematic approach.
