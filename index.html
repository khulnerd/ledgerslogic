ChatGPT did the research —
Here’s the verdict.

Prompt:

I am a small startup based out of USA, I am looking for a professional to handle my bookkeeping work in Quickbooks. Who will be a better fit for this work? CPA (US) or Indian CA certified in Quickbooks?

Criteria CPA (US) Indian CA (QuickBooks certified)
Knowledge of US Accounting Rules (GAAP) ✅ Deep understanding; well-versed in US reporting standards ✅ Good working knowledge (especially if experienced with US clients)
US Tax Integration (for filing prep) ✅ Strong familiarity with US tax compliance, Form 1120/1065, etc. ⚠️ May have limited US tax exposure unless specifically trained
QuickBooks Expertise ✅ Usually proficient, but depends on individual ✅ Often certified and highly efficient (many work full-time on QuickBooks for US clients)
Cost Efficiency ❌ High (CPAs charge $80–$250/hr) ✅ Very cost-effective (usually $15–$40/hr)
Scope of Work Prefers review & advisory; less interested in daily bookkeeping Focused on bookkeeping, reconciliations, reporting — highly process-driven
Availability / Turnaround Time Often limited (CPAs are busy during tax season) Flexible and scalable (many CAs have offshore teams)
